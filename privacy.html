Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Core commitment: this extension does not collect, upload, or store any of your screen recordings or audio. Everything is processed locally in your browser.

1. Data collection and use

We do not collect personally identifiable information, do not include ad-tracking code, and do not record your browsing history. Any video, audio, microphone sound or page content you record is encoded and processed only on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

2. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
tabCaptureCapture the video and audio of the tab you pick, only after you click the extension icon.
activeTabIdentify the tab you are acting on, in order to start the recording.
offscreenKeep the recording session alive in a hidden document so minimizing or switching tabs does not stop it.
storageStore your preferences (video format, microphone toggle) and the current session state locally.
downloadsWrite the finished file to the folder you choose.
notificationsShow the elapsed time and a Stop button in the system notification area while recording.
unlimitedStorageBuffer in-progress recordings locally so a crash does not lose the take.

Microphone is accessed only through the standard getUserMedia API, and only after you enable it in the settings page.

No host permissions are requested. The extension does not read the pages you visit.

3. Third-party services

None. No SDKs, no analytics, no trackers. All data stays private and offline.

4. Contact

Questions about this policy: vantersy2025@gmail.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 12 日

核心承诺：本扩展不收集、不上传、不存储用户的任何屏幕录制内容或音频。所有处理均在您的本地浏览器中完成。

1. 数据收集与使用

我们不收集任何个人身份信息，不包含任何广告追踪代码，不记录您的浏览历史。您录制的视频、音频、麦克风声音以及网页画面， 均仅在您的本地设备上编码和处理，绝不会通过互联网上传至我们的服务器或任何第三方服务器。

2. 权限说明

权限用途
tabCapture仅在您主动点击扩展图标后，捕获您所选标签页的画面和声音。
activeTab识别您当前操作的标签页，作为启动录制的依据。
offscreen在隐藏文档中维持录制会话，确保最小化窗口或切换标签页时录制不中断。
storage在本地保存您的录制偏好（视频格式、麦克风开关）以及当前录制状态。
downloads将录制好的视频文件保存到您选择的本地位置。
notifications录制过程中在系统通知区显示计时与「停止录制」按钮。
unlimitedStorage录制期间在本地临时缓存视频数据，防止因磁盘空间不足导致录像丢失。

麦克风：只有您在设置页主动开启后，才会通过浏览器标准的 getUserMedia 请求；不开启则完全不访问。

不申请任何主机权限，不读取您访问的网页内容。

3. 第三方服务

本扩展不包含任何第三方 SDK、分析工具或数据追踪器。您的所有数据完全私密、完全离线。

4. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有疑问：vantersy2025@gmail.com
